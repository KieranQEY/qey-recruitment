Accessibility statement

QEY Recruitment is committed to making this website usable and accessible to as many people as possible. The site is designed to work on mobile, tablet and desktop screens, uses clear headings and readable text, supports keyboard navigation for standard links and form controls, and includes labels for form fields and alternative text for meaningful images.

We know accessibility is an ongoing process and some content or third-party features may not yet meet every user’s needs. If you have difficulty using this website or need information in another format, please contact kieran@qeyrecruitment.co.uk or call 07858 937430. Please tell us which page or feature is causing difficulty and we will do our best to help.

We aim to improve the site over time and welcome feedback about accessibility.
