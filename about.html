Our story

Why QEY exists.

After more than 15 years in recruitment, including over a decade working in healthcare recruitment across agency and in-house settings, our founder Kieran Jefferies saw how easily recruitment can become focused on filling a vacancy quickly.

But in care, the right match matters. Residents deserve consistency and dignity. Care teams deserve colleagues they can rely on. And candidates deserve an honest conversation about the role, the workplace and whether it is right for them.

QEY Recruitment was created to bring a more personal, partnership-led approach to permanent healthcare recruitment across Northern Ireland. We want to help providers build stable teams and help local people find meaningful, long-term careers in care.

We’re a small, hands-on consultancy. That means you speak directly with us, get clear communication and work with someone who wants to understand the bigger picture — not just the job title.
